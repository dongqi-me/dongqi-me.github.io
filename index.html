About

Hello, thank you for visiting my site! 👋

I am a Ph.D. candidate (doctoral researcher) funded by the European Research Council, under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Vera Demberg at Saarland Informatics Campus, Saarland University. I was also a visiting Ph.D. student at the University of Edinburgh in 2024, working under the guidance of Prof. Dr. Mirella Lapata. To contribute to the NLP community, I joined the ACL Rolling Review Team as a technical member, primarily responsible for ARR website updates. Prior to my Ph.D. studies, I earned a Master’s degree in Computer Science at the University of Amsterdam, under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Khalil Sima'an at the Institute for Logic, Language and Computation (ILLC).
