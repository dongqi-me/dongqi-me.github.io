About

Hello, and thank you for visiting my site! 👋

My Ph.D. was made in Germany at Saarland University, where I conducted research under the guidance of Professor Vera Demberg, with support from the European Research Council (ERC). My dissertation was rated opus eximium, the highest possible evaluation.

During my Ph.D., I was a visiting scholar at the University of Edinburgh, where I worked with Professor Mirella Lapata. Prior to my doctoral studies, I earned an M.Sc. in Computer Science from the University of Amsterdam under the supervision of Professor Khalil Sima'an at the Institute for Logic, Language and Computation (ILLC).

Alongside my research, I contribute to the NLP community as a member of ACL Rolling Review (ARR) organizing team, primarily helping to maintain the ARR website.
